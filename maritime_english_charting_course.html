⚓ Mission Briefing · Αποστολή

You are the navigation team of the M/V AEGEAN STAR. Follow English directions to plan a safe training route from Point A to Point E.

Learning goals

  • Understand basic nautical directions in English.
  • Identify compass directions and course angles.
  • Plot waypoints on a simplified chart.
  • Give and repeat bridge instructions.
  • Calculate distance and voyage time.

Σύνδεση με τη Ναυσιπλοΐα

Πορεία (course), ανεμολόγιο (compass rose), ναυτικό μίλι (NM), κόμβος (knot), σημείο πορείας (waypoint) και βασικός σχεδιασμός διαδρομής.

Maritime distinction: Charting / plotting a course = χάραξη πορείας. Chartering = ναύλωση.

Training only: The chart uses an imaginary grid and is NOT a real nautical chart. Real passage planning also requires official updated charts, depths, hazards, traffic and applicable regulations.

🧭 Compass Directions · Σημεία του ορίζοντα

Listen / Read / Respond

“Steer a course of zero-nine-zero degrees.”
Πλεύσε με πορεία 090° (ανατολικά).

“Alter course to one-eight-zero degrees.”
Άλλαξε πορεία σε 180° (νότια).

“Proceed to the next waypoint.”
Προχώρησε στο επόμενο σημείο πορείας.

“Maintain your present course.”
Διατήρησε την τωρινή πορεία σου.

In marine radio communication, pronounce course digits separately: 090° → zero-nine-zero.

🎙️ On the Bridge · Επικοινωνία στη γέφυρα

Work in pairs. One student is the Master; the other is the Officer of the Watch. Read the dialogue aloud and swap roles.

Master: “What is our present course?”
Officer: “Our present course is zero-nine-zero degrees.”
Master: “What is our next waypoint?”
Officer: “Our next waypoint is Waypoint B, four nautical miles ahead.”
Master: “Alter course to zero-zero-zero degrees at Waypoint B.”
Officer: “I will alter course to zero-zero-zero degrees at Waypoint B.”
Master: “What is our destination?”
Officer: “Our destination is Point E.”
